Cabin HUD privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Cabin HUD shows a cockpit-style flight display using your iPhone's own GPS. It is built to work offline in airplane mode, and it does not collect any data about you.

The short version

  • No data collected. We do not collect, store or receive any of your information.
  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • No tracking or analytics. The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No third parties. We do not sell or share data with anyone.

Location

Cabin HUD uses your iPhone's location to show your position, speed, altitude, flight phase and arrival time. Your location is processed only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

If you allow it, the app keeps using location in the background, so a flight you have started keeps recording when you lock the screen or switch apps. iOS shows a location indicator while this happens. Tap Stop GPS in the app to end it, or change location access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Cabin HUD.

Your logbook

Your flight track, marks, settings and logbook are stored only on your device. They leave your device only when you choose to export them, for example as a GPX or logbook file through the iOS share sheet, and they go only where you send them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Optional flight number lookup

If you type a flight number and tap Look up while you have internet, the app sends only that flight number to free public flight route databases (adsbdb.com, with hexdb.io as a backup) to find the departure and arrival airports. No location, name or other personal information is sent. Like any internet request, those services can see your device's IP address. If you don't use this feature, the app makes no internet requests at all.

Children

Cabin HUD does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.

Disclaimer

Cabin HUD is for entertainment and information only. It is not for actual flight navigation, aviation or safety use, and it must not be used to operate an aircraft or to make any safety decision.

GPS data may be inaccurate, delayed or unavailable, especially inside an aircraft. Altitude, speed, position, flight phase and arrival times shown here can be wrong.

Follow crew instructions and airline rules on device use at all times.

Cabin HUD is provided as-is, with no warranty. Use it at your own risk.
